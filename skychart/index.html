All apps · Each night runs from an hour before sunset to an hour after sunrise. Hours more than two days ahead are faded because forecasts get less reliable further out. Darker blue is better on every row except temperature. Darkness is calculated in the browser from low-precision Sun and Moon positions (accurate to about a degree, so times are ±10 minutes); white means the Sun is above −6°. Transparency is a rough proxy from surface humidity and visibility, not column water vapour. There is no seeing row: Open-Meteo has no seeing forecast. The aurora row uses NOAA's 3-hourly Kp forecast (about three days ahead). The Kp needed is a rough guide by latitude: in South Wales a camera can pick up aurora low in the north from about Kp 6, and Kp 7 or more is usually needed to see it by eye. The last forecast for each site is kept, so the page still opens with no signal. To install on an iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android use the Install app button or the browser menu.
